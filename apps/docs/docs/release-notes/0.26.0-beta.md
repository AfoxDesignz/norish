# v0.26.0-beta

## Summary

This release moves Norish to Node.js 26 and the latest version of every dependency, opens your Library, recipes, cookbooks and groceries up to 14 times faster on a slow phone, makes the Docker image about 30% smaller, and lets an administrator cancel a running background job. AI now keeps the ingredient catalogue tidy by itself, with Undo on everything it does, and a recipe line like "zout en peper" names both foods. It also fixes several security issues, so update soon. Coming from 0.24 or earlier, update to 0.25.1 first: see the [upgrade notes](#upgrade-notes).

## Security

Update as soon as you can. This release fixes:

- **Video links:** a recipe's video link could pass command-line options to yt-dlp, which let a signed-in user run code on the server ([GHSA-732x-jh4x-wg8j](https://github.com/norish-recipes/norish/security/advisories/GHSA-732x-jh4x-wg8j)). Only http(s) links reach yt-dlp now.
- **Recipe pictures and videos:** any signed-in user could add or delete pictures and videos on any recipe, and an upload could write files outside the uploads folder. Only someone who can edit a recipe can change its media now.
- **Private pictures:** any signed-in user could open another household's recipe, step and cookbook pictures by their address, and a shared cache in front of Norish could hand them to anyone. They are shown only to people who can open the recipe or cookbook now, and never kept by a shared cache.
- **Recipe imports:** an import link could name a local file or a server other than a website (`file:`, `gopher:`), and a link written with a backslash could pick up one site's sign-in tokens and send them to another. Imports take only http(s) links now, read the way a browser reads them.
- **Site authentication:** a header token was sent to every other site a page import loaded scripts from, and never with the page itself. It now goes only to the site it is for, with the new Obscura image (see the upgrade notes).
- **Export:** a server admin whose role was taken away could still export every recipe on the instance until they signed out.
- **Sign-in rate limits:** behind more than one reverse proxy, everyone shared one sign-in rate limit. List your proxies in `TRUSTED_PROXIES` to give each visitor their own (see the upgrade notes).

## Features

### AI keeps your ingredients tidy

When AI is set up, it looks at every new ingredient by itself, moments after a recipe, grocery or pantry item adds it, and makes its answer: it merges the ingredient into the food it is, files it under a parent, gives it a clean name, splits a name that is several foods, or deletes it when it is not a food, like a tool or a fragment of a recipe line. Recipe lines and groceries keep their text either way.

**Changed by AI** on the Ingredients page lists what AI did in the last month, with **Undo** on each change. A change AI was not sure of comes first, flags its ingredient, and offers **Keep** as well. An undone change is never made again, and AI leaves alone an ingredient you marked distinct, renamed or gave a parent. Ingredients AI could not reach are asked about again every night. Suggestions, Confirm all, Ask AI, Check with AI and Fix catalogue are gone. See [Ingredients](../groceries/ingredients.md#ai-keeps-the-catalogue).

### Lines that name several foods

A recipe line like "zout en peper" or "boter of olie" points at each food it names, for the pantry, Add to groceries and nutrition. "zout en peper" puts salt and pepper on the list, each with the line's amount. "boter of olie" counts as in the pantry when you keep either, and otherwise puts butter on the list. Nutrition counts the first food of an "or" line and every food of an "and" line. The line shows its first food's icon.

Typed on the list, as a recurring grocery or in the Pantry, "zout en peper" becomes one entry per food. "boter of olie" becomes its first food on the list and both foods in the Pantry. A food whose own name holds an "and", like "mac and cheese", stays one food.

### Delete any ingredient

**Delete** now works on any ingredient, also one a recipe or grocery uses. The dialog says how many recipe lines and groceries keep their text without it, and how many pantry items go with it. A line or grocery whose text names other foods, like "zout en peper" under a deleted "kruidenmix", then points at them. Removing a translation moves what used it to another of the ingredient's names. An ingredient someone added that nothing has used for three months is deleted by itself; one from the catalogue never is. Groceries can repeat yearly ("yearly", "jaarlijks"), so a yearly purchase keeps its ingredient. See [Ingredients](../groceries/ingredients.md#deleting).

### Cancel a running background job

An administrator can stop a running job from the job monitor. An AI request it is waiting on stops at once, and other work stops before its next step. The job shows as Cancelled instead of retrying on its own, and **Retry** runs it again if it was cancelled by mistake. See [Admin settings](../configuration/admin-settings.md#cancelling-a-job).

### Moving between pages

Pages now slide in from the side you are heading, and the other way when you follow a back link. Recipes, Groceries and Calendar slide in the order they sit in the navigation bar, and a recipe's colour fades in and out behind its page. Signing in or creating an account with a password lifts the sign-in screen away and brings the app up. If your device is set to reduce motion, pages change without animation.

### Faster page loads

Opened from a link or a reload, a recipe, a shared recipe, a cookbook, your groceries and the Pantry now show their content in under a second on a slow phone connection, 6 to 14 times sooner than before:

| Page          | Before |   Now |
| ------------- | -----: | ----: |
| Recipe        |  6.9 s | 0.8 s |
| Shared recipe |  5.1 s | 0.9 s |
| Cookbook      |  6.4 s | 0.8 s |
| Groceries     | 10.0 s | 0.8 s |
| Pantry        | 10.2 s | 0.7 s |

The stylesheet is 16% smaller and pages no longer include the settings screen's text, so the first frame comes sooner. Today's meals on the dashboard and the calendar's days come with the page too, and Norish works out "today" in your device's time zone.

Your Library, a cookbook's recipes and the settings page now come with the page as well, and so does what used to fill in seconds later: your avatar, the hearts on your favourites, allergy flags, a recipe's rating, grocery prices and aisles, and the Pantry's suggestions. On the same slow phone:

| Page                      | Before |   Now |
| ------------------------- | -----: | ----: |
| Library                   |  6.7 s | 0.7 s |
| Cookbook recipes          |  6.7 s | 0.7 s |
| Recipe rating and hearts  |  7.5 s | 0.7 s |
| Grocery prices and aisles |  7.0 s | 0.6 s |
| Pantry suggestions        |  6.9 s | 0.6 s |
| Settings                  |  5.9 s | 0.7 s |

A page that comes with its content is a few kilobytes larger, so the Library's first frame lands about 80 ms later than before, and that frame is your Library.

Your browser keeps your Library filters in a cookie, so the server draws the Library you left. Pages open the same way in the installed app.

Tapping Recipes or Groceries in the navigation bar shows the page's skeleton at once, as the other pages already did, instead of waiting for the server.

Measured on a Pixel 7 profile with a 1.6 Mbps connection and its processor slowed four times, as the median of 15 loads.

## Fixes and Improvements

- Ingredient names are read clean: stray brackets, prices and notes are no part of a name, so "gyoza vellen )", "gyoza vellen ((ontdooid))" and "12 gyoza vellen" are all "gyoza vellen", one translation instead of three. A translation you type in an ingredient's panel is stored clean too.
- A line like "Voor de salade:" names no food. It used to match a food, here lettuce.
- A typed grocery that names no food, like ")", stays on the list without adding an ingredient.
- A seasoning, like "taco seasoning", is filed under mixed spices instead of coating. Ingredients filed under coating that way move by themselves on the first start.
- A short word inside an ingredient's name no longer files it under a food: "olie om te bakken" is not tea ("te" in Spanish and Italian), and "raw king prawns" is not apple. A food the name ends with is still its parent at any length, so "rode ui" stays under onion.
- A new ingredient named for a food and its cut, like "mango blokjes" or "chicken strips", is filed under that food without a question on Changed by AI, as is one followed by a note, like "greek yogurt for topping" or "tonijn uit blik". A product named after a food, like "onion powder" or "chili flakes", is still a question. Older ingredients are filed again on the first start.
- When AI reads an ingredient as a food of its own, like "peanut butter", it takes away a parent the ingredient's name gave it, here butter. Changed by AI lists it as "No longer under butter", with Undo. AI sees the parent an ingredient has, and keeps one that is the plain food it is made of or sold as, so "chocolate whey" stays under whey and "Italian herbs" under herbs.
- AI no longer merges an ingredient into a food that is only spelled like one of its names, like "rode pesto" into red port. It is shown the foods spelled a letter or two apart, nearest first, and picks one or none. A food spelled exactly like one of its names is still taken without asking.
- An ingredient AI reads as several foods is split even when it also carries one of their spellings, like "light cooking cream or non-fat Greek yogurt" holding "light cooking cream". That spelling gets an ingredient of its own, and the recipe lines and groceries naming it go with it.
- The Ingredients page no longer lists an ingredient only groceries or the pantry use, like "toilet paper", and AI no longer asks about one, so it can no longer delete one as not a food and leave its grocery without an aisle or price. It is listed, and AI may ask about it, once a recipe uses it.
- Saving a recipe keeps the food of every line you did not change.
- A Recipe Archive keeps the foods each recipe line names, so an imported line points at the same ingredients as the one you exported, also a line naming several.
- Switching between Recipes, Groceries and Calendar opens each page at the top, instead of at the scroll position of the page you left. Going back, with the browser or a page's back link, still returns you to where you were.
- The navigation bar shows again on the next page you open. It could stay hidden after you had scrolled down.
- Going back to the Library after opening a recipe mid-scroll returns you to where you were. The Library used to remember your place only once scrolling had stopped.
- A cookbook no longer shows, for a moment, recipes your Library filters hide. It used to load its recipes without your filters first, then again with them.
- On a phone, the Groceries switch between the list and the Pantry shows icons only, like the Library's grid and list switch.
- Cookie [site authentication](../configuration/site-authentication.md) tokens are sent with page imports, so a recipe that only shows to signed-in visitors imports. The page renderer had been ignoring them.
- The Edit button on the CalDAV settings card opens the editor again.
- Deleting a store can remove its grocery items again: the "Delete items" choice could not be selected.
- Notes in the planner can be moved to another day again: the note editor shows its date field.
- The Open link button in the recipe share panel opens the shared page again.
- Filter chips, timer chips and the library type switch work with the keyboard and screen readers.
- Norish no longer hangs at startup when an import was waiting in the queue.
- The calendar opens on today again, instead of sometimes weeks in the past.
- A meal moved or renamed while its first CalDAV sync was still running now reaches the calendar with the change.
- Scrolling the calendar while offline no longer hides the plans it already shows.
- A setting changed while an earlier change was still saving no longer flips back for a moment.
- Stores keep a fixed order in the store manager, and a Store dragged past several others no longer snaps back to an earlier place.
- A recipe enrichment that finished while its page was opening no longer shows as in progress.
- Pasted ingredient lists keep their amounts when the lines start with a bullet or a dash.
- Perplexity now uses Perplexity's Agent API; the Sonar API Norish used was retired on September 27.
- The recipe parser runs on Python 3.15, with its dependencies updated, security fixes included.
- Dependencies are updated to their latest stable releases.
- The unused image optimization endpoint (`/_next/image`) is no longer served.
- A deleted account is signed out everywhere at once, not on its next request.
- Sign-in rate limits hold when many requests arrive at the same moment.
- A new user signing in with SSO while registration is closed sees the sign-in error page instead of a raw error.
- Background jobs and realtime recover by themselves after a Redis restart, and Norish shuts down promptly after one.
- A recipe import or calendar sync that failed and is due to retry now retries as soon as its wait is over. Before, the retry did not run until another import or sync was started, even after a restart.
- The Docker image is about 30% smaller to download.
- A Mela, Paprika or Mealie import with a very long time field no longer stalls the import.
- An identity provider that was down when Norish started no longer needs a restart of Norish: OIDC sign-in works as soon as the provider is back.
- A planned meal edited just as its calendar sync started reaches the calendar with the edit.
- Norish shows your saved data sooner when you open it.
- Pages show a placeholder in their real shape while loading, and phones get the phone calendar from the start.
- A cookbook you opened while online shows its recipes offline too. Before, it showed none.

## Upgrade notes

- **The ingredient catalogue is cleaned up on the first start.** The first start of this release does it by itself, before Norish takes requests: seeded names lose stray marks, translations that carry an amount, a bracket or a preparation are folded into their clean name together with the recipe lines and groceries that used them, ingredients take their clean name unless you named them, and joined ingredients like "zout en peper" are split into their foods, also ones marked distinct. With AI on, AI is then asked about every ingredient nobody has decided about. There is nothing to do, but on a large catalogue the first start can take a few minutes.
- **Recurrence words.** If an administrator edited the recurrence words, they are replaced once by the shipped ones, which now know years and seven more languages. Edits made after this release stay.
- **Ingredient words.** Edited [ingredient words](../configuration/admin-settings.md#ingredient-words) get none of the new words: the joining words ("and", "or" and their translations), so lines naming several foods are not split on such a server, the cuts and forms (`forms`) and the note words ("for", "voor"). `servedWith` is now called `endsBefore`, and edited words keep theirs under the new name. Add the new words, or restore the defaults.
- **`POST /api/v1/groceries` answers with a list**: one grocery for each food the name names, so "zout en peper" answers with two.
- **Ingredient resolution prompt.** AI now reads a new ingredient's name in one request, and Norish looks its answer up in the catalogue. The answer has a new shape: what the name is (`action`) and a list of the foods it names (`foods`), which Norish checks. If you edited the **Ingredient resolution prompt**, compare yours with the new default under **Settings => Admin => AI & processing => Prompts**, or restore the default: an edited prompt still describes the old answer, and an answer that breaks the new shape twice leaves its ingredient flagged. The question now also names the parent an ingredient is filed under (`Filed under: whey`), and the default asks for a plain food a shopper buys as `parent`, never a category. The second question, which lists foods by number, is now also asked when AI's names only find foods spelled a letter or two apart from them.
- **Clean the name is gone** from Automatic for new ingredients, as AI names every new ingredient now. Draw icons for new ingredients stays. See [New ingredients](../configuration/ai-provider.md#new-ingredients).
- **Node.js 26.** Norish now runs on Node.js 26. The Docker image already includes it. If you run Norish from source, upgrade to Node.js 26 and pnpm 12, and install uv 0.13 or later, which installs the recipe parser's Python 3.15, before updating.
- **Back up Redis first if you might roll back.** The background job queue moves to a new major version with no way back. If you might return to an earlier Norish, back up Redis before updating.
- **Perplexity models.** Perplexity's models are now its Agent API presets: Fast, Low, Medium, High and Extra high. A Sonar model you picked earlier no longer works: if you use Perplexity, pick a preset in the AI settings after updating.
- **OIDC callback URL.** The OIDC callback moves to `/api/auth/callback/oidc`. In your identity provider, replace the redirect URI `https://<your Norish>/api/auth/oauth2/callback/oidc` with `https://<your Norish>/api/auth/callback/oidc`; until you do, OIDC sign-in fails.
- **OIDC ID tokens are verified.** Norish checks each ID token against your identity provider's published signing keys. A provider that signs ID tokens with HS256 no longer works; give it an RS or ES signing key. Signing out of Norish still leaves you signed in at your identity provider.
- **Signing in no longer links accounts.** Signing in with a second provider whose email matches your account is refused with "This email is already in use."
- **New start command.** The image starts the server with `node --import tsx /app/apps/web/server/index.ts`. If you override the container's command, update it.
- **Two health checks replace `/api/v1/health`.** `/api/v1/health/live` is for restarts, and the image's healthcheck now calls it; `/api/v1/health/ready` is for traffic (Kubernetes readiness probes, load balancers) and answers 503 while the database or Redis is unreachable. `/api/v1/health` is gone: if a probe of yours calls it, point it at one of these. See [Server & runtime](../configuration/server-runtime.md#health-checks).
- **Recipe API: pictures are in `images`.** `GET /api/v1/recipes/{id}` no longer returns `image`, and `POST /api/v1/recipes` ignores it. Read and send a recipe's pictures as `images`; the first one is the picture the recipe leads with. Search and URL import results still carry `image`, the first picture. Recipes keep their pictures: one stored the old way, from before recipes had several, becomes the first picture in `images` when you update.
- **Updating from 0.24-beta or earlier.** Update to 0.25.1-beta first, start it once and let its background jobs finish, then update to 0.26. 0.26 no longer converts data from older releases: recipe images in the flat `/recipes/images/` folder of 0.15.4-beta and earlier, units and AI prompts saved before 0.20, auto-tagging and allergy settings from 0.19 and earlier, and ingredients, grocery links and store preferences from before 0.25.
- **New Obscura image.** Set the `obscura` service in your compose file to `norishapp/obscura:0.2.4-norish.2`. It sends a site authentication header token only to the site the token is for. With an older image, page imports send no header tokens.
- **New setting `TRUSTED_PROXIES`.** If Norish sits behind more than one reverse proxy, for example Cloudflare in front of Nginx Proxy Manager, list the proxies' IPs or CIDR ranges in `TRUSTED_PROXIES`. Without it, everyone shares one rate limit for signing in and session checks. See [Server & runtime](../configuration/server-runtime.md#networking--origins).
