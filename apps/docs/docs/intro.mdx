## What is Norish?

Norish is a recipe manager, with the abillity to do meal planning and make grocery lists.
The whole instance is live, an import someone makes will automatically show up for you as well.

> **The name** comes from Nora (our dog) + dish. Coincidentally, it also sounds
> like "nourish".

## Core features

<FeatureGrid>
  <Feature icon={<ArrowDownTrayIcon />} title="Import from anywhere">
    A link imports on its own. With an AI provider, so do videos from TikTok, Reels and Shorts, and
    photos of a recipe.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<CalendarDaysIcon />} title="Plan and shop">
    Plan meals on a calendar that syncs over CalDAV, and share one grocery list, recurring items
    included.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<ChartPieIcon />} title="Know what's in it">
    Nutrition worked out from the ingredients, metric and US conversion, and with AI, allergy
    warnings.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<BoltIcon />} title="Live for the whole household">
    Recipes, groceries and plans update for everyone the moment anyone changes them.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<DevicePhoneMobileIcon />} title="On every screen">
    Made for phones first, in light or dark, usable offline, in 15 languages.
  </Feature>
  <Feature icon={<ShieldCheckIcon />} title="Your server, your rules">
    Sign in with a password, OIDC, GitHub or Google. Admins set permissions and settings in the app.
  </Feature>
</FeatureGrid>

:::note
AI feature speed can vary by provider, model, and region.
:::
